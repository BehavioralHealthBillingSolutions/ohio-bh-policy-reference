# Disclaimer

This repository is a working reference maintained by Behavioral Health Billing Solutions, LLC
(BHBS), an Ohio behavioral health billing and credentialing company. It exists to make Ohio
behavioral health rules, manuals, and agreements reviewable as text so that changes between
versions can be compared line by line.

- **It is not legal advice**, and it is not an official publication of the Ohio Department of
  Medicaid, the Ohio Department of Behavioral Health, or any other agency. For any compliance,
  billing, or legal position, rely on the official source: codes.ohio.gov for current law, the
  Register of Ohio and the agency's rule pages for proposed rules, and medicaid.ohio.gov for ODM
  manuals and agreements.
- **Proposed rules are proposals.** Files labeled proposed, draft, BIA, CSI, or ERF are not
  operative and may change or be withdrawn. A comment deadline or a date printed inside a draft
  is not an effective date.
- **Historical files are evidence of what was published at the time**, not current authority.
- **Analysis reflects BHBS's reading** from a billing, EHR-configuration, authorization, and
  claims perspective. It is written for Ohio community behavioral health agencies and may not
  apply to other provider types or states. Items marked ⚠️ Risk Flag identify ambiguity or
  exposure that BHBS considers material; absence of a flag is not a clearance.
- **Text extraction can introduce errors.** Source files record retrieval dates and hashes so
  that any copy can be checked against the official document. Report anything that looks wrong
  by opening an issue.

Terminology used in BHBS analysis: *client, agency, or group* means the organization BHBS serves;
*provider* means the rendering clinician; *member* means the agency's patient. ODM documents use
"client" or "recipient" for the Medicaid member; that usage is preserved inside source text.
