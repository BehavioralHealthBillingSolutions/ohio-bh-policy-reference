# Primary sources and reuse boundaries

Official publications control. Source links and hashes identify evidence; they are not a
license to redistribute all content at the linked location.

| Collection | Official source | Repository treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Ohio statutes and rules | [Ohio Laws](https://codes.ohio.gov/) | Dated working copies and snapshots; verify effective dates. |
| Managed-care agreements | [ODM managed-care policy page](https://medicaid.ohio.gov/resources-for-providers/managed-care/mc-policy/managed-care-policy) | See [agreement register](Managed%20Care%20Agreements/Excerpts/OMISSIONS.md) for verified direct PDFs and omissions. |
| BH manuals | [ODM manuals page](https://medicaid.ohio.gov/resources-for-providers/bh/manuals) and [historical index](https://medicaid.ohio.gov/resources-for-providers/bh/information-for-providers/bh-manuals) | Link-only pending public redistribution-scope confirmation; no full manual archive is included. |
| ODM ERF 236150 proposal | [Official referral PDF](https://dam.assets.ohio.gov/image/upload/medicaid.ohio.gov/Stakeholders%2C%20Partners/LegalandContracts/Rules/BIAERF236150.pdf) | Stable URL has changed bytes; see [SOURCE_STATUS.md](SOURCE_STATUS.md). |
| DBH service rules | [Official draft page](https://dbh.ohio.gov/about-us/rules-and-regulations/rules/draft-rules/requirements-and-procedures-for-behavioral-health-services), [redline PDF](https://dam.assets.ohio.gov/image/upload/mha.ohio.gov/AboutUs/RulesandRegulations/Rules/Draft-Rules/Service-Rules.pdf), [BIA PDF](https://dam.assets.ohio.gov/image/upload/mha.ohio.gov/AboutUs/RulesandRegulations/Rules/Draft-Rules/BIA-Service-Rules.pdf) | Dated proposals and linked comparisons, not effective law. |

Some ODM/DBH landing pages returned 404 during automated retrieval while the direct PDFs
downloaded successfully. This is a retrieval limitation, not a claim that the sources never existed.
Direct agreement URLs were located and downloaded October 15, 2026.

Government-hosted documents may contain third-party actuarial reports, CPT content, assessment
instruments, or other material with separate rights. BHBS grants rights only to its own work.
Omitting identified Milliman reports does not certify every other source component as unrestricted.
