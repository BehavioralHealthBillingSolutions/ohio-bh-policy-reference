## Executive Summary

The October 5 BIA/CSI packet contains **nine proposed rule identifiers** across 62 PDF pages and prints an October 12, 2026 comment deadline. It is marked **DRAFT — NOT YET FILED**. The baseline is the effective Ohio Laws text retrieved October 7, 2026.

Key changes concern MH day-treatment counseling, PSR groups, collateral contacts, SUD assessment/attendance, and authorization dependencies. MH day treatment in October differs materially from September’s MH IOP/PHP model. TBS retains unlicensed qualification pathways. Draft cross-reference errors and residential coverage conflicts need clarification.

The October packet supplies **no fee appendix**. September’s proposed rates are historical scenario inputs, not confirmed October rates. Summaries and likely impacts follow; each rule links to separate full current/proposed text and a diff.

## Changes and Likely Impacts

<a id="policy-5160-8-05"></a>

### 5160-8-05 — Licensed-professional services and supervision

**Classification:** Substantive proposal

Supervision and documentation are clarified; proposed (B)(7) permits immediate availability via real-time audio-visual technology when allowed by the licensing board. Proposed (D)(1)(c)(iii) names CANS assessment coverage effective July 1, 2027. Proposed (D)(3) points future service limits to the 5160-1-31 appendix/Master List, and (E)(2) adds explicit recreational exclusions. Current (G)(2)(d) already pays general-supervision trainees at 85% of the supervising practitioner rate; the October draft retains that formula. It does not establish a new across-the-board trainee rate increase versus the current rule.

**Likely impact:** Agencies need board-specific supervision evidence and service-specific claim rules. Scope, payer implementation, and CANS rollout must be resolved before changing production claims. Forecast trainee reimbursement by supervisor tier and service; do not treat the retained 85% formula as a newly earned uplift.

**Next action:** Credentialing/compliance: map supervision modality by licensing board. Billing/system owner: retain separate trainee direct/general-supervision logic and stage CANS changes for verified implementation.

⚠️ Risk Flag. The September draft contained an additional 72.25% general-supervision clause that is absent from the October attachment. Do not carry that conflicting September clause into the updated scenario.

### 5160-27-01 — Eligible agencies, rendering providers, and supervisors

**Classification:** Substantive proposal

Proposed (A)(1) updates OhioMHAS to DBH and expressly ties agency services to the services/supports on DBH certifications and national accreditation standards. Proposed (A)(6) changes the supervisor reference from (D)(1)–(D)(17) to (D)(1)–(D)(16), excluding the listed certified peer supporter from that unlicensed-practitioner supervision reference. The broader (D) list still contains a certified peer supporter at (17). The BIA describes this rule mainly as an agency-name/typo update, but these text differences warrant operational review.

**Likely impact:** Agency certification scope and rendering-provider supervision assignments require reconciliation. A peer supervisor appearing on the general list does not by itself establish eligibility to supervise an (A)(6) QMHS/CMS under the narrowed cross-reference.

**Next action:** Credentialing/clinical leadership: reconcile program certifications, accreditation scope, and supervisor assignments; confirm the intended treatment of peer supervision with ODM/DBH.

⚠️ Risk Flag. Distinguish the BIA narrative description from the actual proposed (A)(1) and (A)(6) text; do not dismiss these changes as clerical without review.

### 5160-27-02 — Coverage, CPST transition, collateral contacts, and authorization dependencies

**Classification:** Substantive proposal

Current (B)(3) expressly covers CPST; the October proposal removes that standalone service from the listed coverage framework and lists TBS/PSR, ACT, MH day-treatment hourly/per-diem, nursing, SBIRT, testing, psychiatric diagnostic evaluation, psychotherapy, and peer support as potentially subject to limits in the 5160-1-31 appendix. Proposed (H)(1) replaces the current minimum-QMHS treatment-plan standard with a licensed-practitioner reference to 5160-27-01(D)(1)–(D)(6), while allowing supervised unlicensed participation. Proposed (K)(6) enumerates nonreimbursable supervision activities. Proposed (K)(7) permits same-organization collateral contact across different lines of business with consent/justification, treatment-plan purpose, documented benefit, and no concurrent billing by both providers.

**Likely impact:** CPST activities need a clinical service/activity crosswalk rather than automatic rebilling under another code. EHR workflows need consent, line-of-business identification, collateral purpose/outcome, and duplicate-contact edits. PA limits cannot be inferred from this packet because the controlling appendix is a separate package.

**Next action:** Clinical/billing owners: map affected CPST encounters and validate collateral documentation. Compliance: seek clarification on treatment-plan author eligibility. Forecast owner: keep PA utilization assumptions editable until the separate appendix is available.

⚠️ Risk Flag. The (D)(1)–(D)(6) treatment-plan citation excludes later-listed independently licensed practitioners such as LISWs and LPCCs. Obtain clarification before interpreting the citation as an intended restriction. The separate Master List and payer implementation remain unresolved.

### 5160-27-04 — ACT replacement and fidelity-dependent payment

**Classification:** Substantive proposal

The BIA expressly identifies rescission and replacement of ACT. The replacement uses DBH rule 5122-29-29 for service/fidelity requirements, keeps the four-unit monthly structure, adds Master List dependency, and retains ODM payment suspension authority tied to fidelity. Member eligibility is restructured, not simply retained: proposed (D)(1) replaces the current schizophrenia/bipolar/major-depressive-disorder-with-psychosis criterion with any valid and current behavioral health diagnosis on the ICD-10 code-group list, (D)(2) requires documented ANSA training, and (J) now requires at least one direct prescriber contact every three months. The complete replacement text is compared with the effective predecessor below; reduced rule length does not imply removal of incorporated DBH requirements.

**Likely impact:** Agencies need certification/fidelity evidence and coordinated enrollment/disenrollment records. Bundling and outpatient exclusivity continue to affect revenue outside ACT. Preserve clinical eligibility and team-category unit controls in the forecast.

**Next action:** ACT program/compliance owner: reconcile the incorporated DBH standard, certification, fidelity monitoring, enrollment notice process, and maximum unit categories.

⚠️ Risk Flag. The replacement contains mismatched paragraph references: (H) refers to fidelity in (J)(1), but (J) is prescriber contact; (I)(3) refers to categories (L)(1)/(L)(2), which are nonbillable activities; (F)(2) refers to notifying ODM of enrollment after discharge. Treat these as draft clarification items.

### 5160-27-06 — MH day-treatment hourly and per-diem group counseling

**Classification:** Substantive proposal

Current (B) uses a 2.5-hour daily split for hourly versus per diem and allows specified same-day services and some cross-provider billing with PA. The October draft reframes the service as the group-counseling component of multimodal MH day treatment: hourly reimbursement is capped at ten hours per week; full per diem requires at least 3.5 hours/day and 10.5 hours/week; at least one hour forty-five minutes but less than 3.5 hours permits 50% of the applicable provider-type per diem. Two consecutive weeks below 10.5 hours trigger rescission of existing authorization, with lower-level reimbursement not exceeding ten hours/week. Proposed (C)(2)(a)(i) delays licensed-only rendering to July 1, 2027; the 1:12 ratio remains. Proposed (D)(3) bars hourly and per-diem same-day reimbursement for the same member by any billing provider.

**Likely impact:** Revenue modeling must use delivered group-counseling minutes and weekly attendance, not total program attendance. Staffing costs require a transition-date scenario. The October service title and structure supersede the September draft assumption that the modeled MH services should be labeled IOP/PHP.

**Next action:** Forecast owner: build MH hourly/per-diem counseling scenarios with the 1.75/3.5-hour half/full thresholds, ten-hour hourly cap, 10.5-hour weekly minimum, two-week attendance logic, 1:12 group ratio, and July 1, 2027 licensed-only transition. Billing: prepare cross-provider same-day review.

⚠️ Risk Flag. Proposed (C)(2)(a) cites paragraph (A)(2) of rule 5160-27-08, which does not exist in the proposed 5160-27-08 (its (A) has no subparagraphs; practitioner qualifications moved to (C)(2) for TBS and (D)(3) for PSR), and the between-ten-and-10.5-hours treatment/reimbursement boundary needs clarification. Dollar rates are not included in this October package.

### 5160-27-08 — TBS and PSR activity remapping, qualification tiers, and groups

**Classification:** Substantive proposal

The October draft assigns shared treatment-planning, psychoeducation, and crisis-prevention activities to both TBS and PSR, then separates TBS evidence-informed symptom interventions from PSR functional rehabilitation. Proposed (C)(2) retains TBS eligibility for licensed or unlicensed practitioners with the degree pathway or a high-school diploma plus three years relevant experience; the current rule already has those alternatives. Proposed (D) adds functional-deficit member criteria and expressly includes licensed or unlicensed PSR rendering practitioners with population-specific training. Current (D)(3) prohibits group PSR; proposed (F)(4) allows it at 1:6. Proposed group TBS is less than 10.5 hours/week with a 1:12 ratio. PA limits move to the separate appendix. Proposed (F)(1) also drops the current (D)(1) sentence barring separate TBS/PSR payment during IHBT enrollment unless prior authorized; any replacement limit would come from the 5160-1-31 appendix.

**Likely impact:** TBS is not globally licensed-only under this attachment. Forecast eligible staffing by service and documented qualifications. PSR groups create a modeled service option but require clinical fit, ratio controls, group duration/units rules, and verified fee/PA implementation; activity remapping must remain clinically supported.

**Next action:** Clinical/credentialing owners: map activities, eligibility, documented experience and population training. Forecast/system owner: add separate TBS and PSR groups with distinct ratios and threshold logic.

⚠️ Risk Flag. Proposed (E) defines collateral contact as “meaningful interaction with a recipient who play a significant role in a recipient’s life,” rather than clear third-party language (the current rule’s (B) says “contacts individuals who play a significant role in a medicaid recipient’s life”). Proposed (F)(4) cites (B)(1)(b)/(B)(1)(c), which do not exist in the revised shared-activity outline. Proposed (F)(1) still excludes SUD residential payment, conflicting with proposed 5160-27-09(C)(3)(h).

### 5160-27-09 — SUD assessment, per-diem attendance, residential bundles, and ASAM evidence

**Classification:** Substantive proposal

The draft adds separate SUD level-of-care and treatment-planning assessments for ASAM 1, 2.1, and 2.5 apart from psychiatric diagnostic evaluation. Proposed (C)(2) defines group-counseling intensity and per-diem time, excludes breaks, and includes July 1, 2027 full/half daily thresholds: IOP 3h1m/1h31m and PHP 4h1m/2h1m. Proposed (E) requires dimensional risk ratings, treatment-goal linkage, baseline/interval validated measures, and outcome/discharge evidence. The residential per diem bundles listed core services while proposed (C)(3)(h) describes TBS, PSR, MH day treatment, ACT, and IHBT as eligible services, unlike the current noncovered list; September’s (C)(3)(h) read “considered non-covered,” so the October flip to “eligible for reimbursement,” with no change to the MH-side exclusions, reads as a drafting error rather than a policy reversal. Outside-per-diem providers must be unaffiliated and the service essential, medically necessary, and unable to wait for discharge. Proposed (F)(4) adds attendance downgrade and no additional same-day SUD group counseling; (F)(5) adds admission/discharge and same-organization transition billing rules.

**Likely impact:** Revenue forecasts need counted group minutes, interruption/break time, attendance downgrade logic, assessment assumptions, residential bundle exclusions, and separate outside-provider controls. Documentation changes require structured ASAM and outcome-measure evidence. Residential carve-outs cannot be modeled as confirmed collectible revenue while contradictory MH restrictions remain.

**Next action:** SUD clinical/billing owners: build ASAM and assessment documentation checks, group-time/break tracking, attendance and transition edits. Forecast owner: stage 2027 minute thresholds, half-rate scenarios, and residential carve-outs as unresolved alternatives.

⚠️ Risk Flag. Proposed 5160-27-09(C)(3)(h) conflicts with continued SUD-residential exclusions in 5160-27-06(D)(1), 5160-27-08(F)(1), and ACT exclusivity. Other errors include the withdrawal-management reference to (B)(2)(e), which does not exist (the WM components sit at (C)(2)(e)), and the IOP downgrade in (F)(4)(b), which says less than an average of nine to nineteen hours and applies the nine-hour adult trigger to adolescents even though (C)(2)(a) defines adolescent IOP as six to nineteen hours per week. Do not implement these literally without ODM clarification.

### 5160-27-10 — SUD targeted case-management limits

**Classification:** Revised limits and terminology

The October proposal retains the core targeted-case-management activities, excludes QMHS and certified peer supporters as rendering practitioners, and retains residential bundling. Current (E) expressly requires PA for TCM when a member is enrolled in ACT or IHBT; proposed (E) instead refers additional scope, duration, frequency, and co-occurring-service limits to the 5160-1-31 appendix. Agency name and member terminology are also updated.

**Likely impact:** Removal of the specific PA sentence is not evidence that ACT/IHBT-related TCM becomes unrestricted. Qualified rendering-provider controls and residential bundling remain relevant.

**Next action:** Billing/authorization owner: retain existing applicable payer controls until the finalized rule, appendix, and implementation instructions establish the replacement requirements.

⚠️ Risk Flag. The October package does not supply the new appendix limits; keep ACT/IHBT authorization and bundling outcomes unresolved in the proposed scenario.

### 5160-27-14 — Peer-service authorization and MRSS exception

**Classification:** Substantive proposal

The proposal removes the current explicit more-than-four-hours/day PA sentence and references limitations in the 5160-1-31 appendix instead. The 1:12 peer-group ratio remains. Proposed (E)(1)(c) adds an MRSS exception for unrelated peer services already actively furnished by another billing provider before initiation of the regional mobile crisis response. Admission/discharge language for residential, inpatient, and PRTF exceptions changes from admission and discharge to admission or discharge.

**Likely impact:** Agencies need proof of preexisting peer-service relationships, separate billing providers, and the crisis initiation timeline before relying on the MRSS exception. Deleted four-hour language does not establish unlimited peer coverage.

**Next action:** Peer program/billing owners: add documentation fields and a prebilling exception review for MRSS overlap; monitor the separate PA appendix and payer instructions.

⚠️ Risk Flag. The newer text does not provide replacement daily/hour limits or establish the operational claims edit for the MRSS exception.
