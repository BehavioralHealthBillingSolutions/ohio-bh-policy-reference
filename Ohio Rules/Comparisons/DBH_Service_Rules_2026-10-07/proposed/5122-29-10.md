# 5122-29-10 — Crisis intervention service

## Rule Text

(A)  As used in this rule, "provider" means a provider described in rule 5122-25-01 of the Administrative Code to whom Chapter 5122-25 of the Administrative Code applies.

(B)  Crisis intervention service is an interaction with an individual in response to a crisis or emergency situation the individual is experiencing.

(C)  Crisis intervention service includes:

(1)  An urgent evaluation of the following elements when clinically indicated:

(a)  Understanding what happened to initiate the crisis and the individual's response or responses to it;

(b)  Risk assessment of lethality, propensity of violence, and medical/physical condition including alcohol or drug use;

(c)  Mental status;

(d)  Information about the individual's strengths., coping skills, and social support network, including face-to-face contact with family and collateral informants; and

(e)  Identification of treatment needs and appropriate setting of care.

(2)  The development of a crisis plan to de-escalate the crisis, stabilize the individual, restore safety, provide referrals and linkages to appropriate services, and coordinate with other systems.

(D)  A provider of crisis intervention service is to have current certification in first aid and cardio-pulmonary resuscitation (CPR) and be trained in de-escalation techniques.

(E)  When an individual appears to be medically unstable, the individual is to be referred to a medical facility or emergency medical service is to be called.
