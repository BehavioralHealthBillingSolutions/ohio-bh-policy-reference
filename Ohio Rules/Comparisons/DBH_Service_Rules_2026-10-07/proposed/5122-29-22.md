# 5122-29-22 — Referral and information service

## Rule Text

(A)  As used in this rule, "provider" means a provider described in rule 5122-25-01 of the Administrative Code to whom Chapter 5122-25 of the Administrative Code applies.

(B)  "Referral and information service" means responses, usually by telephone, to inquiries from individuals about services in the community. Referral may include contacting any agency or a provider to secure services for the individual requesting assistance.

(C)  Referral and information service is to be planned and coordinated with other health and human service providers, and is to satisfy both of the following conditions:

(1)  Have a mechanism to compile information about services available in the service system and community; and

(2)  Have processes to determine whether individuals referred were able to access services, were satisfied with the services, or experienced any problems with the referral source. This information is to be used to determine if particular providers are to continue to be used as referrals for individuals seeking services. All state and federal confidentiality laws are to be adhered to in this process.

(D)  The provider is to ensure access and availability of referral and information service including satisfying both of the following conditions:

(1)  Having a published telephone number for the referral and information service, including a published telephone number for special telephone services for the hearing impaired; and

(2)  Ensuring access and availability to the referral and information service for individuals whose primary means of communication is a language other than English, and for persons with communication impairments (such as speech, language, or hearing disorders, access to telecommunication relay services (TRS)), and for persons with visual impairments. With respect to individuals with communication impairments, the provider is to ensure access to a telephone communication relay service (TRS). The services associated with TRS include, but are not limited to, text to speech relay and signing to speech relay.

(E)  Each call and contact is to be logged and include the date, time, and person answering the call or contact.

(F)  A referral and information service is not a hotline service, and is not intended to replace the crisis assistance function of a hotline service.
