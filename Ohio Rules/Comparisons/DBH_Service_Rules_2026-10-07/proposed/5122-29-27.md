# 5122-29-27 — Supplemental behavioral health services

## Rule Text

(A)  As used in this rule:

(1)  "Mental illness" has the same meaning as in section 5119.01 of the Revised Code.

(2)  "Provider" means a provider described in rule 5122-25-01 of the Administrative Code to whom Chapter 5122-25 of the Administrative Code applies.

(B)  "Supplemental behavioral health services" means services other than those specifically listed in this chapter. Supplemental behavioral health services may include representative payeeship, outreach, screening, education, and other supportive behavioral health services and may be offered by a variety of entities, including YMCAs, churches, or county family and children first councils.

(C)  A provider applying for initial or renewal certification of supplemental behavioral health services under rule 5122-25-02 or 5122-25-04 of the Administrative Code, or seeking to add supplemental behavioral health services to the menu of services the provider provides under rule 5122-25-03 of the Administrative Code, is to include all of the following information in their application:

(1)  The name of each specific supplemental behavioral health service to be provided along with a brief description of each specific service; and

(2)  A letter from the board of alcohol, drug addiction, and mental health services of each alcohol, drug addiction, and mental health service district where the provider intends to provide supplemental behavioral health services that describes the board's support for the provider's provision of the services.

(D)  A provider of supplemental behavioral health services is to do all of the following:

(1)  Ensure that the provision of the services meets the appropriate standards or regulations;

(2)  Ensure that staff providing the services have participated in orientation or training regarding basic information about mental illness, emotional disturbance, and substance use disorders, and know how to obtain assistance from the behavioral health system if needed; and

(3)  Develop mechanisms to solicit and receive feedback about the quality of the services from individuals served.
