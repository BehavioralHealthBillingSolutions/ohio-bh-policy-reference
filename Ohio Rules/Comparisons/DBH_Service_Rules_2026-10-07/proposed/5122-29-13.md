# 5122-29-13 — SUD case management services

## Rule Text

(A)  Substance use disorder (SUD) case management services means those activities provided to assist and support an individual in gaining access to medical, social, educational, and other services essential to meeting the client's basic human needs. Case management services may include interactions with the client's family members or other individuals or entities. Case management activities may include, but are not limited to, assessment, referral, monitoring, and follow-up.

(B)  SUD case management services are to be delivered by individuals who are familiar with the community resources in which they provide the services.

(C)  SUD case management services are to be provided according to a client's comprehensive individualized treatment plan developed in accordance with rule 5122-27-03 of the Administrative Code.

(D)  SUD case management services do not include any of the following:

(1)  Transportation;

(2)  Waiting with an individual for appointments at social service agencies, court hearings, and similar activities;

(3)  Direct services to which the client has been referred such as medical, educational, or social services; or

(4)  Internal quality assurance activities, such as clinical supervisory activities and/or case reviews/staffing sessions.
