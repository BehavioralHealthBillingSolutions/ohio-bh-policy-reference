# 5122-29-19 — Consultation service

## Rule Text

(A)  As used in this rule, "provider" means a provider described in rule 5122-25-01 of the Administrative Code to whom Chapter 5122-25 of the Administrative Code applies.

(B)  "Consultation service" means a formal and systematic information exchange between a provider and a community partner (including, but not limited to, a school system, court, family member of an individual receiving or to receive behavioral health services, or other person who is not an established provider of the individual receiving or to receive behavioral health services) that is directed towards the sharing of information on how to support, intervene, and address concerns or behaviors of the individual receiving or to receive behavioral health services, including facilitating the availability of appropriate behavioral health services for that individual.

(C)  Consultation may be focused on the clinical condition of an individual served by another system or focused on the functioning and dynamics of another system. Consultation related to the clinical condition of an individual served is to be provided by staff qualified according to paragraph (D) of this rule.

(1)  The provider is to periodically survey other community systems to determine behavioral health consultation needs that may be desired by the systems, individuals, or families being served by those other systems

(2)  The provider is to maintain a record of all consultation services provided, including the name of the individual or system to whom the service was provided, the nature of the consultation, and the outcome of the consultation.

(D)  Consultation service is to be provided and supervised by staff who are qualified according to rule 5122-29-30 of the Administrative Code.
