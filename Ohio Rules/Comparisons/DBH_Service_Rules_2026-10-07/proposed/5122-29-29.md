# 5122-29-29 — Assertive community treatment services

## Rule Text

(A)  As used in this rule:

(1)  "ACT certified provider" means a provider that is certified under this rule to provide assertive community treatment services.

(2)  "ACT team" means a multidisciplinary team of practitioners, all of whom are qualified in accordance with rule 5122-29-30 of the Administrative Code and have specialty training in the provision of assertive community treatment, who provide team-based services in accordance with the ACT model and are employees or contractors of an ACT certified provider.

(3)  "Approved fidelity review" means a fidelity review conducted by an independent validation entity recognized by the department.

(4)  "Provider" means a provider described in rule 5122-25-01 of the Administrative Code to whom Chapter 5122-25 of the Administrative Code applies.

(B)  Assertive community treatment (ACT) services are intended for individuals diagnosed with a severe and persistent mental illness that need high service intensity. ACT services are also provided to the family members and other support systems of such individuals. Individuals receiving ACT services may also have a co-occurring diagnosis.

(C)  The purpose of ACT services is to provide the necessary interventions and support to maximize recovery and promote success in employment, housing, and stability in the community. The service is available twenty-four hours a day, seven days a week.

(D)  ACT services providers are to employ one or more ACT teams to implement one or more programs that meet the minimum fidelity criteria described in this rule using the tool for measurement of assertive community treatment (TMACT) or the dartmouth assertive community treatment scale (DACTS).

(E)  Before submitting an application for initial certification under this rule, a provider is to undergo an approved fidelity review and achieve a minimum fidelity score of 3.0.

(F)  Once a provider becomes an ACT certified provider, each of its ACT teams is to undergo ongoing approved fidelity reviews. Each fidelity review is to occur within twelve months of the team's most recent fidelity review.

(G)  Within three years of each ACT team's initial approved fidelity review, the team is to achieve a minimum fidelity score of 4.0. If a team fails to achieve that standard or fails to achieve a minimum fidelity score of 4.0 at any fidelity review conducted after the three- year period, the provider responsible for the ACT team is do one of the following:

(1)  Submit a waiver request to the department in accordance with rule 5122-25-10 of the Administrative Code; or

(2)  Disband the ACT team because of fidelity noncompliance.

(H)  As part of a waiver request submitted under paragraph (G)(1) of this rule, the provider responsible for the ACT team is to submit all of the following:

(1)  A copy of the ACT team's most recent fidelity review report.

(2)  A proposed timeframe by which the ACT team will achieve the minimum 4.0 fidelity score.

(3)  A fidelity action plan the ACT team has developed in collaboration with an independent validation entity recognized by the department that addresses all problem areas identified in the most recent fidelity review report.

(4)  Documentation that the provider has established an internal steering committee responsible for guiding and monitoring the implementation of the fidelity action plan described in paragraph (H)(3) of this rule. The committee is to include, at a minimum, the provider's ACT team leader, clinical director, and quality improvement or quality assurance director.

(5)  If at the time the waiver is requested the ACT team is subject to a previously-granted waiver, documentation that that ACT team has undergone a new fidelity review prior to the expiration of the previously-issued waiver and a copy of the results of that review.

(I)  If an ACT team has developed a fidelity action plan described in paragraph (H)(3) of this rule, the provider responsible for the ACT team is to (a) ensure that plan is addressed in the provider's quality improvement plan developed under rule 5122-28-03 of the Administrative Code and (b) monitor progress toward achieving plan objectives.

(J)  The department may request that a certified ACT provider undergo a new fidelity review at any time based on one or more findings of noncompliance with this chapter.

(K)  Prior to termination of ACT services due to an individual's unexpected non-participation, an ACT team will attempt at least two contacts per month with the individual until one of the following is the case:

(1)  The ACT team has attempted to contact the individual on at least two occasions each month for a period of ninety days and the individual has not responded. An ACT team is to document each attempt at contacting an individual in the individual's record; or

(2)  The individual notifies the ACT team of the individual's desire to discontinue the services.

(L)  ACT services are to be provided and supervised by staff who are qualified according to rule 5122-29-30 of the Administrative Code. A particular staff member may provide only those components of the service for which the individual possesses the appropriate qualifications or credential.
