## Rule Text

(A) Mental health intensive outpatient (IOP) and partial hospitalization (PHP) services are a multimodal service as described in rule 5122-29-06 of the Administrative Code. Mental health IOP and PHP are intensive, structured, goal-oriented, distinct and identifiable treatment services that address the individualized mental health needs of the recipient. Mental health IOP and PHP are clinically indicated by assessment. The environment at this level of treatment is highly structured. The purpose and intent of mental health IOP and PHP is to stabilize, increase or sustain the highest level of functioning. Recipients receiving mental health IOP or PHP will have a treatment plan that includes explicit and measurable goals that define patient improvement.

(B) For the purpose of medicaid reimbursement, this rule is applicable to the group counseling component of IOP and PHP services.

(1) These group counseling services include but are not limited to the following:

(a) Development and strengthening of interpersonal and social competencies, including problem-solving, conflict resolution, and other skills necessary for the maintenance of functional relationships in home, school, community or vocational settings;

(b) Identification, development, and facilitation of positive coping mechanisms for the regulation and management of emotions and behavior;

(c) Managing mental health and behavioral symptoms to enhance vocational or educational opportunities or independent living skills; and

(d) Psychoeducational services including instruction and training of the recipient to increase their knowledge and understanding of their psychiatric diagnosis(es), prognosis(es), treatment, and recovery, improve skills, and favorably affect their outcomes.

(C) Service requirements.

(1) As defined in rule 5122-29-06 of the Administrative Code, mental health IOP and PHP are distinguished by treatment intensity. Treatment intensity (i.e. number of hours per week) and duration is individualized and designed to meet the needs of the recipient and will be adjusted according to the recipient's response to therapy and ability to participate effectively.

(a) Mental health IOP group counseling. Reimbursement for mental health IOP is limited to ten hours per week and may be reimbursed on an hourly basis;

(b) Mental health PHP group counseling is reimbursed as a per diem rate. Mental health PHP group counseling service provision has to meet the following criteria:

(i) Be provided for 3.5 hours or more per day; and

(ii) Be provided for a minimum of 10.5 hours per week.

(a) If at any time during the episode of care, a recipient fails to complete the minimum weekly service requirement of 10. 5 hours per week of group counseling for two consecutive weeks, any existing service authorizations will be rescinded, but services may still be reimbursed at a lower level of care not to exceed ten hours per week.

(2) Mental health day IOP and PHP services has to:

(a) Be provided by a licensed practitioner as described in rule 5160-27-01 of the Administrative Code;

(b) Maintain a staff to recipient ratio that does not exceed 1:12.

(D) Limitations.

(1) Reimbursement for mental health IOP and PHP services will not be made while the recipient is enrolled in assertive community treatment (ACT), or a substance use disorder (SUD) residential treatment facility.

(2) Mental health IOP and PHP group counseling services as well as allowable same day individual and non-counseling group services, may be subject to limitations, which will be described on the master list on the Ohio department of medicaid website as referenced in rule 5160-1-31 of the Administrative Code. Limitations may apply to initiation and continuation of services, frequency, scope, and duration of services, and allowances for services to be provided on the same day.

(3) Mental health IOP and mental health PHP group counseling services for adults will not be reimbursed when delivered on the same day to the same recipient by any billing provider except for recipients ages eighteen or older who are enrolled in OhioRISE as referenced in rule 5160-59-02 of the Administrative Code .

(E) Providers must adhere to documentation requirements set forth in rules 5160-1-27 and 5160-8-05 of the Administrative Code.
