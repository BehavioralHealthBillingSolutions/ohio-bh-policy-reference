## Rule Text

(A) Mental health day treatment programs are multimodal services as described in rule 5122-29-06 of the Administrative Code. Mental health day treatment programs are intensive, structured, goal-oriented, distinct and identifiable treatment services that address the individualized mental health needs of the recipient and that have been clinically indicated by assessment. The environment at this level of treatment is highly structured. The purpose and intent of mental health day treatment programs are to stabilize, increase or sustain the highest level of functioning. Recipients receiving mental health day treatment will have a treatment plan that includes explicit and measurable goals that define patient improvement.

(B) For the purpose of Ohio department of medicaid (ODM) reimbursement, this rule refers to the group counseling component of mental health day treatment programs.

(1) These group counseling services include but are not limited to the following:

(a) Development and strengthening of interpersonal and social competencies, including problem-solving, conflict resolution, and other skills necessary for the maintenance of functional relationships in home, school, community or vocational settings;

(b) Identification, development, and facilitation of positive coping mechanisms for the regulation and management of emotions and behavior;

(c) Managing mental health and behavioral symptoms to enhance vocational or educational opportunities or independent living skills; and

(d) Psychoeducational services including instruction and training of the recipient to increase their knowledge and understanding of their psychiatric diagnosis(es), prognosis(es), treatment, and recovery, improve their skills, and favorably affect their outcomes.

(C) Service criteria.

(1) As defined in rule 5122-29-06 of the Administrative Code, mental health day treatment is distinguished by treatment intensity. Treatment intensity (i.e. number of hours per week), treatment duration and the delivery of additional individual treatment services are individualized, designed to meet the needs of the recipient and adjusted according to the recipient's response to therapy and their ability to participate effectively in all treatment elements. For the purposes of ODM reimbursement, the group counseling services of mental health day treatment can be delivered as an hourly or per diem service.

(a) Mental health day treatment hourly group counseling. Reimbursement for mental health day treatment hourly is limited to ten hours per week and is reimbursed on an hourly basis;

(b) Mental health day treatment per diem group counseling is reimbursed as a per diem rate. Mental health day treatment per diem group counseling service provision has to meet the following criteria:

(i) Be provided for 3.5 hours or more per day; and

(a) If a recipient attends a session for at least one hour and forty- five minutes but for less than 3.5 hours, the rendering provider may be reimbursed at fifty per cent of their provider type per diem rate for that session.

(ii) Be provided for a minimum of 10.5 hours per week.

(a) If at any time during the episode of care, a recipient fails to complete the minimum weekly service criteria of 10.5 hours per week of group counseling for two consecutive weeks, any existing service authorizations will be rescinded, but services may still be reimbursed at a lower level of care not to exceed ten hours per week.

(2) Mental health day treatment hourly and per diem services are:

(a) Provided by a licensed practitioner or unlicensed mental health practitioner as defined in paragraph (A)(2) of rule 5160-27-08 of the Administrative Code.

(i) Effective July 1, 2027, mental health day treatment hourly and per diem group counseling services can only be provided by a licensed practitioner as described in rule 5160-27-01 of the Administrative Code.

(b) Maintain a staff to recipient ratio that does not exceed 1:12.

(D) Limitations.

(1) Reimbursement for mental health day treatment hourly and per diem group counseling services will not be made while the recipient is enrolled in assertive community treatment (ACT), or a substance use disorder (SUD) residential treatment facility.

(2) Mental health day treatment hourly and per diem group counseling services may be subject to limitations, which will be described on the appendix to rule 5160-1-31 of the Administrative Code, which is also posted to WWW.Medicaid. Ohio.gov as "ODM Master List for Prior Authorization." Limitations may apply to initiation and continuation of services, frequency, scope, and duration of services, and allowances for services to be provided on the same day.

(3) Mental health day treatment hourly and per diem group counseling services cannot be reimbursed when delivered on the same day to the same recipient by any billing provider.

(E) Providers have to adhere to documentation criteria set forth in rules 5160-1-27 and 5160-8-05 of the Administrative Code.
