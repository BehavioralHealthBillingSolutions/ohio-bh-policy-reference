SUBJECT:
Revision of Behavioral Health Coverage Rules
The  Medicaid behavioral health coverage rules listed below will be filed to: 1) fulfill five-year
rule revision requirements, 2) update and clarify Medicaid policy, and 3) strengthen fraud, waste,
and abuse policy.
As noted below, some rules are being revised, and others rescinded and then proposed as new
rules. This is because so much of the rule is being revised it is more appropriate for it to be
proposed as a new rule.
The following rules will be proposed for amendment:
5160-8-05, “Behavioral Health Services-Other Licensed Professionals” will be proposed
for amendment to update rule references, supervision definitions, and service
authorization language.
5160-27-01, “Eligible Provider of Community Behavioral Health Services” will be
proposed for amendment to correct a text error and update the name of a sister agency.
5160-27-03, “Reimbursement for Community Behavioral  Health Services” will be
proposed for amendment to update reimbursement policy for four services and add new
language regarding the child and adolescent needs and strengths assessment.
5160-27-10, “Substance use Disorder Targeted Case Management” will be proposed for
amendment to update the name of a sister agency and to add new language regarding
service authorization.
5160-27-11, “Behavioral Health Nursing  Services” will be proposed for amendment to
update terminology and policy concerning group nursing services.
5160-27-14, “Behavioral Health Peer Support Service” will be proposed for amendment
to update  the name of a sister agency and to add new language regarding service
authorization.

The following rules will be proposed for rescission and adoption as new:
5160-27-02, “Coverage and Limitations of Behavioral Health Services” will be
proposed for rescission and adoption as new to add new language regarding
service authorization and to remove language concerning peer recover services
and other behavioral health services.
5160-27-04, “Mental Health Assertive Community Treatment Service” will be
proposed for rescission and adoption as new to remove language concerning
service disenrollment, eligible provider requirements, and documentation
requirements.  New language clarifies existing Medicaid policy.
5160-27-06, “Mental Health Intensive Outpatient and  Partial Hospitalization
Services” will be proposed  for rescission and adoption as new to change the
name of the rule (previously was “therapeutic behavioral group service hourly and
per diem”) and to remove existing language and add new language which clarifies
Medicaid policy regarding the services.
5160-27-08, “Mental Health Therapeutic Behavioral Services and Psychosocial
Rehabilitation” will be proposed for rescission and adoption as new to add new
language addressing service coverage limitations and clarify the components that
make up the two services.
5160-27-09, “Substance Use Disorder Treatment Services” will be proposed for
rescission and adoption as new to add new language clarifying service
requirements concerning intensive outpatient and partial hospitalization programs.
New language also addresses service reimbursement policy, documentation
requirements, and service coverage limitations.
