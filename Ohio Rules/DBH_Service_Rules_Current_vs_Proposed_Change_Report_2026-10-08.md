## Executive Summary

DBH submitted 18 amended service rules to CSI on October 7, 2026; the supplied BIA lists October 14, 2026 as the public comment deadline. The 50-page rule packet remains a proposal marked Draft – Not for Filing. Current authority was retrieved live from Ohio Laws on October 8. No effective date, Medicaid fee schedule, code/modifier transition, or quantified fiscal impact is established by this packet.

The principal operational changes are CPST-to-PSR service redesign under 5122-29-17; adult-focused short-term TBS under 5122-29-18; a three-day weekly minimum for MH day treatment; peer-run organization governance/access/funding requirements; and ACT fidelity waiver/remediation requirements. The day treatment rule remains titled MH day treatment, so a September MH IOP/PHP assumption should not be carried into this DBH scenario without reconciliation.

**Comparison boundary:** This is a complete current-law-to-October-proposal comparison of the 18 supplied rules. The exact September DBH rule text was not recovered; therefore a verified September-to-October rule-by-rule redline is not included. BIA descriptions of stakeholder revisions are reported separately as agency statements, not substituted for that missing baseline.

### 5122-29-01 — Purpose and applicability

**Classification:** Clarification

The current purpose provision refers to services certified by the department. The target ties the chapter to the provider definition in 5122-25-01(A). This is an applicability clarification; it does not independently establish Medicaid payment eligibility.

**Likely impact:** Check which BHBS client agencies and service locations fall under DBH certification.

**Next action:** Credentialing: verify applicability against each agency certification.

### 5122-29-03 — General services

**Classification:** Substantive amendment

Current (B) and target (C) retain Appendix A credential requirements. Target (C)(1)-(2) adds explicit ASAM third edition level 2.1/2.5 requirements for SUD IOP/PHP. Current (C)(4) and target (D)(4) both require the comprehensive assessment within 30 days; the deadline is retained. Target (D)(1), (D)(6) and (E)(1) expands assessment/treatment language to include gambling and tobacco/nicotine and adds ASAM to standards of care. The 1:12 group counseling ratio remains unchanged: current (D)(3), target (E)(3).

**Likely impact:** Update assessment and treatment-plan templates without treating the retained 30-day deadline or 1:12 ratio as new requirements. Keep SUD IOP/PHP credential and ASAM checks distinct from MH day treatment.

**Next action:** Clinical operations: review assessment templates and SUD IOP/PHP standards; credentialing: obtain the governing Appendix A.

⚠️ Risk Flag. The supplied packet references Appendix A but does not include it. Target (A) calls 5122-25-01 a chapter. Do not infer a revised credential matrix or unrestricted telehealth coverage from this draft.

### 5122-29-06 — Mental health day treatment service

**Classification:** Substantive amendment

Current (E) sets 2–7 hours per program day. Target (E) retains 2–7 hours per day and adds at least three days per week. Target (E)(5)-(6) adds treatment/referral capacity for identified medical, psychological or psychiatric needs and ongoing participant monitoring, including level-of-care changes and treatment-plan adjustments. Target (G) specifies an interdisciplinary team and limits each staff member to components appropriate to their credential. The October title and body are MH day treatment, not MH IOP/PHP.

**Likely impact:** Model at least three program days per week, monitor attendance and staffing capacity, and retain a separate MH day treatment scenario. The daily hours are not new. The draft does not supply a fixed staffing ratio, Medicaid unit calculation, or new rate.

**Next action:** Forecast owner: revise the proposed day treatment schedule and monitoring assumptions; clinical operations: map staffing and referral capacity.

⚠️ Risk Flag. Reconcile DBH 5122-29-06 with the October ODM 5160-27-06 proposal before replacing the forecast service model. Neither proposed packet establishes an operative payment policy.

### 5122-29-07 — Forensic evaluation service

**Classification:** Substantive amendment

Target (B)(19) adds competency to waive Miranda rights. Current (D)(1) requires the examiner relationship to be explained orally and in writing; target (D)(1) requires an understandable oral explanation, documentation of the process and understanding in the report, and permits a written explanation. **Likely impact:** Update forensic report templates and consent/confidentiality explanation documentation. These certification requirements do not by themselves make court-related services Medicaid-covered.

**Next action:** Forensic program lead: review templates and referral authority.

⚠️ Risk Flag. Target (C)(3) names a regional psychiatric hospital without clearly stating the required order/request; Request clarification of the referral authorization language.

### 5122-29-09.1 — Substance use disorder qualified residential treatment program (QRTP) for youth

**Classification:** Substantive amendment

Target (A)-(C) restates provider/certification scope. Current (B)(3)(f)(i) points to the older JFS resource; target (C)(3)(f)(i) uses the DCY trauma-model resource. Current (B)(7) already requires prehire checks and clearance before presence in the program under 5122-30-31. Target (C)(7) changes the check reference to 5122-26-06(F)(2), while retaining eligibility under 5122-30-31. The 24/7 nursing/clinical availability, six-month aftercare and under-14-day exception are retained rather than newly created.

**Likely impact:** Review workforce screening, approved trauma model, onboarding and aftercare documentation. Staffing availability and aftercare costs should remain in the baseline where already required.

**Next action:** QRTP program/HR leads: validate the screening workflow and trauma-model approval.

⚠️ Risk Flag. The BIA describes additional DCY-guided background checks, but the rule body points to 5122-26-06 and 5122-30-31. Resolve the exact governing checks before implementing a new exclusion list.

### 5122-29-13 — SUD case management services

**Classification:** Substantive amendment

Current (A) requires assessment, referral, monitoring and follow-up as minimum activities; target (A) says activities may include those functions. Target (C) explicitly ties services to a comprehensive individualized treatment plan. Current (B)(1)-(2) says transportation/waiting does not in itself constitute case management; target (D)(1)-(2) lists transportation and waiting as excluded. **Likely impact:** Document the case management intervention and treatment-plan relationship, and separate transportation/waiting time from claimed service time. Do not assume every removed example is forbidden or every restored example is reimbursable.

**Next action:** Billing/clinical operations: revise note review and time segregation controls.

⚠️ Risk Flag. BIA question 10 says group settings are allowed, but 5122-29-13 does not expressly state group delivery. Confirm group billing with ODM and the applicable payer; avoid treating the BIA as a payable code/modifier instruction.

### 5122-29-16 — Peer run organization service

**Classification:** Substantive amendment

The current rule sets four broad organizational principles. Target (C)(1) requires recovery supports and certified peer services outside a comprehensive ITP and outside Medicaid/private-insurance third-party reimbursement; grants/contracts/appropriations do not count as reimbursement for this paragraph. Target (C)(3) requires at least 51% lived-experience governing membership, with a caregiver alternative for specified missions. Target (C)(4)-(5) requires predictable published hours and broad community access; (D)-(E) adds mobile-unit and telehealth conditions.

**Likely impact:** Separate grant-funded peer-run organization activity from reimbursable clinical peer services. Review governance composition, access restrictions, published hours and mobile-unit ownership.

**Next action:** Agency leadership/finance: map funding streams; credentialing: verify the certification model.

⚠️ Risk Flag. Do not reduce all agency peer-service revenue to zero. The restriction concerns the peer-run organization service specified in this rule; separately certified Medicaid peer services require their own analysis.

### 5122-29-17 — Psychosocial rehabilitation service (PSR)

**Classification:** Service replacement within amended rule

Current 5122-29-17 is CPST. Target (B)-(C) defines PSR and requires agency capacity for all nine listed activity categories, with individualized delivery tied to a comprehensive ITP. It carries forward several CPST-like functions while revising their purpose and evidence requirements. Target (D) permits high-school-diploma staff with relevant training only for (C)(1)(b), (c) and (i). Target (E) permits contracting with another PSR-certified provider; current (D) expressly allows certain delegation to noncertified entities under written agreement.

**Likely impact:** Map actual CPST/TBS/PSR activities, member needs and rendering-provider qualifications; do not relabel claim codes by rule number. Review subcontractors because the target contracting language is narrower than current CPST delegation.

**Next action:** Clinical/billing/credentialing leads: create an activity-by-credential service crosswalk and review subcontractor agreements.

⚠️ Risk Flag. A blanket CPST-to-PSR revenue conversion is unsupported. Agency certification capacity is not the same as each rendering provider eligibility; only three activity categories are explicitly available under the high-school training provision.

### 5122-29-18 — Therapeutic behavioral services

**Classification:** Substantive service redesign

Current (A)-(B) combines TBS and PSR. Target (B) describes TBS for adults with a mental health diagnosis, intensive one-on-one/group outpatient, short-term and focused on high-risk/disruptive/self-destructive behaviors. Target (C)(1)-(2) details interventions and ongoing monitoring. Target (C)(3) uses degree-qualified practitioners or QBHS under 5122-29-30; current (C)(1)(b) expressly allowed high-school education plus three years relevant experience. PSR moves to target 5122-29-17.

**Likely impact:** Review youth currently receiving TBS, long-duration services, activity documentation and staff qualifications. Build alternative service scenarios based on member need and approved service eligibility.

**Next action:** Clinical/billing leads: identify affected youth and long-duration encounters; credentialing: validate QBHS qualifications.

⚠️ Risk Flag. The draft gives no numeric short-term duration or new fixed group ratio. Do not hard-code an age-transition date, duration cap or rate change absent final adoption and payer instructions.

### 5122-29-27 — Supplemental behavioral health services

**Classification:** Substantive certification clarification

Target (C)(1)-(2) expressly requires service names/descriptions and a support letter from the ADAMH board in each district where services are intended. Current (B)-(C) already contemplates board/department approval and certification, but does not state this application checklist in the same form. Target (D) retains standards, staff orientation and service feedback duties.

**Likely impact:** Review multi-district agency services and collect district-specific board support before certification applications/updates.
