i.

enrollment, the member may receive covered benefits that are also covered by 
Medicare. During that time, unless the provider has agreed in writing to an 
alternative payment methodology or different secondary claims payment rate, the 
MCO must adjudicate Medicare secondary claims as set forth in OAC rule 5160-1-

exemptions to the Part B Medicaid maximum policy in accordance with the OAC and 
other guidance issued by ODM.

g. Nursing Facility Payment 
i. 
At a minimum, the MCO must pay nursing facility providers in accordance with the 
Ohio Revised Code, including ORC sections 5165.15 and 5165.158. 
ii. 
The MCO must ensure accurate claims payment to nursing facility providers by 
appropriately modifying payment pursuant to OAC rule 5160-3-39.1 when a member 
has patient liability obligations or lump sum amounts. 
iii. 
The MCO must apply patient liability as an offset against the amount the MCO would 
otherwise reimburse for the claim. If the patient liability exceeds the amount the 
MCO would reimburse, the MCO must process the claim with a payment of $0. 
iv. 
The MCO must not pay for nursing facility services during a member's restricted 
Medicaid coverage period (RMCO). 
v. 
The MCO must utilize HIPAA compliant enrollment files from ODM to determine a 
member's patient liability obligations and restricted Medicaid coverage period. 
h. Nursing Facility Based Hemodialysis (HD) Add-On Payment 
i. 
Purpose: 
 In accordance with Ohio Administrative Code (OAC) rule 5160-13-08, 
beginning on effective date of the rule, MCO will pay an add-on amount 
of one hundred ten dollars per treatment for nursing facility-based (NF-
Based) HD furnished by a dialysis center, in addition to the HD per-visit 
payment amount. 
ii. 
Eligible Individuals: 
 The MCO must reimburse the NF Based HD add-on payment only for 
Members enrolled in the Ohio Medicaid Managed Care Program. The 
MCO must discontinue payment of the add-on when the Member 
becomes eligible for Medicare benefits, consistent with OAC rule 5160-
13-08(C)(2). 
iii. 
Program Timeframe and Funding Limitations: 
 The add-on payment must be available only during State Fiscal Year 
2027 and will expire June 30, 2027, or earlier if ODM determines 
statewide expenditures have reached $2.1 million. ODM will monitor 
expenditures and notify the MCOs when funding has been exhausted or 
the program has expired. Upon ODM notification, the MCOs must 
immediately disable acceptance and payment of claims containing the 
add-on payment.
