 Pursuant to OAC rule 5160-2-65, the MCO must ensure coverage for

"Ohio Solid Organ Transplant Consortium." The review and 
recommendation for coverage is based on criteria established by Ohio 
organ transplant surgeons and authorization from the ODM prior 
authorization unit.
