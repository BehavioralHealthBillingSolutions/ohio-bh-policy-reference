4. Member Requirements 
a. Health Equity 
 
In accordance with 42 CFR 438.206(c), the MCOP must address health care disparities and 
ensure equitable access to and the delivery of services to all members, including those with 
limited English proficiency, diverse cultural and ethnic backgrounds, or disabilities; and 
regardless of gender, sexual orientation, or gender identity. 
 
"Equitable access" for purposes of this Agreement means meeting the standards as defined 
by the National Standards for Culturally and Linguistically Appropriate Services in Health and 
Health Care (https://thinkculturalhealth.hhs.gov/clas ). 
 
In accordance with 42 CFR 438.206(c)(3), the MCOP must ensure that the MCOP, its 
subcontractors, and network providers provide physical access, reasonable 
accommodations, and accessible equipment for members with physical or mental 
disabilities.

Population Health and Quality. 
 
The MCOP must participate in ODM's health equity initiatives as requested by ODM. 
b. Member Information 
 
The MCOP must comply with applicable federal and state laws, and federal regulations 
regarding persons with limited English proficiency (LEP) and persons with disabilities, 
including Title VI of the Civil Rights Act of 1964, Titles II and III of the Americans with 
Disabilities Act, Section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973, Section 1557 of the Patient 
Protection and Affordable Care Act, and 42 CFR 422.2267(a)(2-4) and 422.2267e(31) as well

accommodations for persons with LEP and persons with disabilities. The plan must require 
training of pertinent staff on the process. The plan must be made available for review by 
ODM at ODM's request. 
 
The MCOP must provide written notice of nondiscrimination (i.e., that the MCOP may not
